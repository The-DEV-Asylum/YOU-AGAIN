Local by design

No cloud account, analytics, or remote access. Observation data stays on the AntiStalker and in this app’s current session. Your phone saves only the device address (plus its last known home IP/mDNS name) and refresh preferences; home Wi-Fi credentials, trusted-device keys, and the home position are saved only on the ESP32. Phone GPS sharing is opt-in: while the app is open, fixes go straight to the device, which keeps the latest one in RAM only.

Understanding risk signals

NEARBY means a BLE advertiser was seen. It is not suspicious by itself.

PERSISTENT means a known tracking tag met time and advertisement thresholds.

FOLLOWING means a known tag appeared repeatedly at two user-confirmed locations.

CO-PRESENT is the equivalent multi-location signal for an ordinary BLE MAC address.

 Important limitations

BLE results are heuristic and unauthenticated. They cannot identify an owner or prove intent.

RSSI is not distance — distance estimates are rough and often off by a factor of two. Non-advertising, Bluetooth Classic, proprietary, powered-off, cellular, and GPS-only trackers may be invisible.

A TRUSTED badge proves a device shared its key during your one-time pairing — it never proves who carries it.

If you feel at risk, move to a safe place and contact appropriate local support or emergency services. Do not confront someone based on BLE output.
